Pilot (S1E1)

★ ★ ½

19-08-2026

A very generic first-day-of-school episode save for the smattering of vampires. It sets up all the characters and their future storylines well enough, but is generally a little boring for a pilot. I wish they had lingered on the Stefan-being-the-primary-danger red herring a little longer, for at least another episode, but he is quickly set up as a good reformed vampire unlike his murderous brother. Damon is supposed to be dangerously sexy, I think, though he is acted almost exactly like Devon Bostick's Rodrick. Not that Rodrick does not have his appeal, but it feels out of place in a semi-serious teen drama. I do enjoy this stylistically though; Elena's outfits, the Pete Wentz reference, an extra who shows up for a split second with the most authentic emo hair of all time, all resonate with me deeply. More so than the vampires, funnily enough.
