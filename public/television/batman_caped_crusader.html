Cute ending with Batman realising he really isn't alone, but the show would have benefited from actually developing those relationships instead of merely dictating them. I didn't enjoy this Joker much, but his motivations and seeing himself in Batman was interesting. The impalement really shocked me because how on earth was Batman to know Joker would survive that? And how on earth did he survive that?

Dead Before Dawn (S2E9)

★ ★ ★ ½

04-11-2026

I cannot believe it took this long for Batman to make his suit bulletproof. A cold and calculated Joker is fine, but one with no sense of humor? Maybe next episode I will come around to him. With the amount of bodies that pile up this episode, it is the first time the threat actually feels severe. Maybe now that he's lost him, Batman will actually start seeing Alfred as a person.

Zero Hour (S2E8)

★ ★ ★

04-11-2026

What did they do to Mr. Freeze? Conceptually interesting enough, a body disposal service for the Gotham underworld, but he himself was a let down. Carrie and Batman both being inspired by Grey Ghost is cool, and it would be nice if she was his Robin down the line, but Batman barely has a warm relationship with Alfred as of now, let alone with a child soldier.

Her Dark Reflection (S2E7)

★ ★ ★

03-11-2026

I'm not the biggest fan of this version of Poison Ivy. After Harvey, another villain with a split personality seems pointless. However, I do appreciate the Veronica Lake inspired look. The Riddler has been utterly wasted as just a maniacal crime boss, but he was so annoying I am glad he's dead.

Sudden Fear (S2E6)

★ ★ ★ ★

03-11-2026

Montoya harbouring Harley really took me by surprise. I'm glad to see her again, and I actually really like this take on her character if it would just go deeper. I do think her constant therapy-speak one liners are a bit annoying though. I was a bit shocked Batman was so focused on making Harley snap out of it that he was not able to save that girl. His fear being getting too old to save Gotham is very interesting, both because it brings to mind Batman Beyond, and as it was hinted at in the previous episode, with Batman being personally invested in the success of the life-extending treatment. Scarecrow disappointed me, to be honest.

The Spectral Hand (S2E5)

★ ★ ★ ½

03-11-2026

Bruce is really not convincing to me at all. Corrigan's powers really reminded me of Death Note, but there never is an explanation given as to why they do not work on Batman. He is pretty badly injured by the time he gets to Barbara's home, so I assumed he might end up revealing his identity to her a la Daredevil. This season is really not shying away from violence and gore, that impalement scene was crazy. I think this could be a great show if it was allowed to run long term, but there are only a few episodes left and there is no surety there will ever be a third season.

The Devil's Due (S2E4)

The bloodiest episode so far, I think. The cultists managing to get the better of Batman, cutting him up like that, felt very contrived and out of character. Great seeing Gordon and Batman fighting together and Gordon even publicly defending him. Once again, the Joker teases are annoying because they are all just a few seconds of him watching Batman. Deep Cover (S2E3)

★ ★ ★ ★

03-11-2026

Prison infiltration/escape episodes are always so very fun, and I really enjoyed watching Bruce undercover. I would really like to see a Matches Malone live action adaptation. You also see a bit of Batman's humanity as he seems to genuinely have formed a bond with Des, and feels remorse over his loss. Des calling Batman his best friend rather than expressing a feeling of betrayal just made it that much more tragic. I really don't like this Riddler though, and the Joker teases are getting annoying.

Rocket's Red Glare (S2E2)

★ ★ ★ ½

03-11-2026

Felt like an episode of B:TAS. Some anti-corporate themes, but I feel that with the concept the episode could have been more interesting. The 1940s retrofuturism was really fun though, and it is sweet that Batman ensured Roxy's legacy would live on, and that he destroyed her blueprints. I'm still not sold on this iteration of Bruce Wayne, but it is good to see more of him and his use of that persona.

Brute Force (S2E1)

★ ★ ★

03-11-2026

I was surprised by the relative amount of "gore" and violence in this episode, it feels a lot more adult. Hattie is an interesting portrayal of the media and propaganda, which is ever relevant but perhaps now more than ever. It seems this season will be a lot more serialised, leading up to the Joker. A just fine episode to start the new season, and hopefully it will pick up.

Savage Night (S1E10)

★ ★ ★ ★

03-11-2026

I cannot believe they killed Harvey off already, but his arc was incredibly strong, especially with how Barbara has been tied to it from day 1. I really liked the way he was used as a foil for Batman, and though Alfred says the difference between the two is that Bruce is still in touch with his humanity, it is complicated. At least he calls Alfred by name this time. It is supposed to be a moment of growth, but I mean, come on, this is the man that raised you! Batman pointing that gun at Flass was so tense, because though it is a cemented fact Batman does not kill, he gets real close here and it really emphasises how angry he is, both at the loss of a friend and his own guilt about leading Harvey there. The ending shot of Batman watching Thorne from the skyline was beautiful, and the episode ought to have ended there instead of tacking on a Joker epilogue.

The Killer Inside Me (S1E9)

★ ★ ★ ★

03-11-2026

Why is Batman so mean to Alfred in this show? Show your father some respect. Really interesting that even Bruce being a good friend to Harvey is all an act so he can be Batman. It makes a lot of sense with how deeply he has fallen into that identity and his crusade at the expense of everything else, but I cannot help but compare it to B:TAS Batman, who would never do that. But then again, Bruce and Harvey are never shown to be as close as we are told they are in this show, with the latter having more of a rapport with Barbara. Seeing the tables turn with her as his public defender is bound to be juicy, compounded with his split personality. That just comes out of nowhere here though.

Nocturne (S1E8)

★ ★ ★

02-11-2026

The orphans being called Dickie and Jason is so funny. I wonder if we get a Robin in this show. Natalia is just a carbon copy of Wednesday Addams, and her chasing Carrie through the woods is very creepy. Carrie clinging to Batman's cape on the way back home is very cute. Harvey begins his transformation as Two-Face as well, though the manner in which it is brought about is somewhat underwhelming.

Moving Target (S1E7)

★ ★ ★ ½

02-11-2026

Onomatopoeia is great, but he feels quite wasted here. I liked the clashes in moral views Barbara and Jim have, and it just drives home again the themes of institutional corruption. Her lack of faith in the system and strong hand-to-hand combat skills make me think it is only a matter of time before Batgirl. I spent a long time waiting for Batman to show up in this episode though.

Night Ride (S1E6)

★ ★ ★

02-11-2026

Forgot this was a cartoon for a minute, so I thought it would have to be like Scooby-Doo where the ghost is actually a real estate developer or something. I like that the relationship between Batman and Alfred is being developed, especially since Batman is so short with him sometimes, but the wider themes about classism are heavy handed and poorly done. All the Harvey set up feels out of place with this episode in particular, but I am interested to see this show's rendition of Two-Face.

The Stress of Her Regard (S1E5)

★ ★ ★ ½

02-11-2026

Never in a million years would I have thought of Montoya and Harley as a couple. I really like her design in this, and the way her jester costume contrasts with her serious, intelligent demeanor, though her public face is very bubbly. I expect when she returns it will be with the Joker, though I can't really see this version of her playing sidekick. I also like that she is nuanced here, she still cares deeply about Barbara even when their goals are not aligned and risks her life to save her. Barbara herself is very physically active this episode, even apprehending a runaway, and I wonder if it is a way to ease her into the role of Batgirl? I wish there had been more of Bruce having therapy with Dr. Quinzel prior to the events of this episode, which could have foreshadowed her dark side over time.

The Night of the Hunters (S1E4)

★ ★ ★ ½

02-11-2026

An episode which demonstrates why Gotham needs Batman. The system is so irrevocably broken, and a couple of cops with good intentions will never win over larger agendas. Flass needs to actually die, but it seems like Bullock is also pretty irredeemable in this series. Feels a lot more mature than the previous episodes with references to Batman's fetishes, Firebug's arson fantasies, and explicit death, but it needs to either commit to the tone or not.

Kiss of the Catwoman (S1E3)

★ ★ ★ ½

02-11-2026

I think I can appreciate what the voice is doing now, even though it isn't my favourite. Batman and Bruce both sound very different, but Batman is not a voice he puts on to intimidate his enemies, it seems to be his true voice (and self) as he also uses it with Alfred. Catwoman was a little disappointing and has the ugliest car I have ever seen, but Dr. Quinzel makes an appearance, and as Bruce's therapist, no less! I really hope to see more of her. A more Batman focused episode for sure, contending with his repressed trauma early on, but God the animation style makes kid Bruce seem so creepy.

...And Be a Villain (S1E2)

★ ★ ★ ½

02-11-2026

Clayface's design here is so creepy I thought it was a Scarecrow episode when I first saw him. Basil reminded me of Waldo in Laura, which fits the whole noir atmosphere, but I did really miss Matt Hagen. Montoya is great, and I am glad that she is not only given more focus, but an entire episode. She does seem very similar to Barbara though. I like that the other characters are fleshed out like this, especially when they are women, but so far there has been barely any Batman/Bruce. Better than the first episode, so I am optimistic about the rest of the show.
