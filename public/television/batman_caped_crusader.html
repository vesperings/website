★ ★ ★

01-11-2026

For a moment, I thought Penguin was a drag queen and was completely shocked. Turns out she is just a woman, which is also pretty cool. I was also suprised to hear her make an outright sex joke. Barbara in particular seems like such a strong character already, which makes me wonder if Batgirl will make an appearance. Her disdain of Bruce and admiration for Batman immediaely made me suspect there would be a relationship between them, so on a hunch I checked if Bruce Timm was involved with this show, which he is, so safe to say it is inevitable. At lease she is close in age to Bruce, I suppose. The animation is far less interesting than the poster and credits suggest, and Batman's ears are very long, which is cute, actually. It might take me a while to get used to his voice though.
