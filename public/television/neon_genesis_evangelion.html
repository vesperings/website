Angel Attack (E1)

★ ★ ★ ½

01-11-2026

It is far too early for me to form any opinions yet, but what I can say with certainty is that the animation is beautiful. It is so beautiful, and literally catches your breath at points. I am particularly enthralled by the design of the "angel", though is nature is still a mystery. Thematically, it is interesting that man-made Evas are employed to fight what seems to be a natural, organic being - one called an angel, no less. Other than that, I only have questions. Why did Shinji see Rei on the street? Why can only they pilot the Evas? NERV is certain Shinji can do it, but what makes him special? And what is the angels' motivation?
