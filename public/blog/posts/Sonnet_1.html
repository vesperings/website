Yearning to create, needing to so I could feel human, I decided to seriously attempt writing a poem for the first time in my life. This is the result.

Twin figures guard imperceptible gates

Blazing anubi in the streetlamp glow

Could these be dogs sent to disclose my fate

Who cannot turn back or return to home?


With no choice I make my approach to find

At sudden car headlights one jolt and flee

I realise and laugh in soft delight

They are only foxes, not deities


The remaining cub suckles liquor nips

Panics if pedestrians kick them away

Knowing I ought not I feed it a crisp

Risk raising predator as human prey


I turn, ignore pleas, so guilty and cruel

Better to taste hunger than diesel fuel
