VESPERINGS' OOTDS!

This is essentially just an outfit diary. I don't have a "style" or "aesthetic", I just wear what I want when I want so don't expect anything inspirational lmao. Though I do tend to gravitate towards pieces that are red or black. 99% of the time every outfit is paired with a black mask, and I only ever really switch out my first lobe piercings while the rest remain the same, so I also don't include those in the collages.

22-08-2026

For the first time in a long while, the heat has subsided enough to wear anything but a cotton summer dress. I usually only wear shades of reds and blacks, but this blouse is pretty enough that that I deviate. It appears a lot lighter in the image than it is in reality, though. I still own all my original skinny jeans from the 2010s, but this pair is more recent because I realised I do not any high waisted ones. I purchased them on eBay, but believe they are the Hallie jeans from New Look. The Doc Martens are the Sinclair Max variety, and I adore the platform. These are also second-hand, like probably 90% of what I own. I had inteded to wear my bat shaped locket with this outfit instead, but was not able to find it, so this chunky heart necklace had to do instead. It does feel rather cohesive with the blouse, though. And of course, the tattoo chokers. Obviously, I am aware of the fact that I am not a cartoon character and my identity/personality is not any lesser depending on what I wear, nor am I under any obligation to perform a caricature of myself for the benefit of strangers, but sometimes I still struggle to shake the feeling that I am in a costume, especially since my makeup is often not so dark and smokey these days because it irritated my eyes too. These little pieces of plastic are a light and non-invasive comfort blanket of sorts, which ground me regardless of what I am wearing. With these, whether I am in school uniform or pastels (God forbid), I still feel myself. This outfit really makes me feel like a mean older sister in 2008. Strangely enough, a guy at the mall looked me up and down and said "nice." I hardly ever get comments when I am wearing anything elaborate, so this basic outfit catching several people's attention surprised me.
