Absolute Batman #1 (2024)

★★★★

01-11-2026

I really enjoyed this! I expected all muscles and brute force with not much else, but was pleasantly surprised. Far tamer so far than I had been led to believe, and a very different iteration of Bruce's character.

[...]

Is it even still Bruce Wayne if he is A) not a billionaire and B) not an orphan? I am almost certain his mother will end up dying soon anyway though and he is still young enough for it to be backstory material (he is only 24!), but I have no idea how he accomplished his suit and weapon collection without unlimited funding.

Either way, I think making him working class is the funniest possible twist to his character. It also creates a lot of implications for him, as he can no longer use his wealth and privilege to cover himself, though as Alfred says, he is also able to hide in plain sight now precisely because he is a nobody.

The contrast between the brutality of how Batman deals with the Party Animals and his apparent enjoyment of it, and his no-kill rule and tenderness with his mother is very interesting, and I hope more is done with that.

An Alfred that is not inherently loyal to Bruce is also very interesting, but the reveal at the end of his original target being the Joker suggests they will team up sooner rather than later, though likely with a very different dynamic.
