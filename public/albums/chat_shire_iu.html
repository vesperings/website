MY REVIEW

Of course I only put this on to listen to Twenty-Three on my 23rd birthday, but the entire album is a solid gem. I definitelt prefer the more upbeat and jazzy tracks, but can also appreciate the others because the qulity is so good. I am inspired to read the book Zezeis based on so I can understand both the song and the controversy surrounding it better.
